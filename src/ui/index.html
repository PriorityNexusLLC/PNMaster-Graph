        Connections staged from HOOT's SEC evidence. Nothing enters the graph until you approve it here, and every approved row is re-validated.

        How to review (full guide: Obsidian → HOOT/Review Guide)
        1. Read the card: who · relationship · what; green active = a filing in the last ~15 months; click filing to see the SEC proof.
        2. Approve only if: right person (check the filing if the name looks odd) · the filing says it · it matters to your investigation. Unsure? Skip; nothing is lost.
        3. Domains for every new entity: people show suggestions marked *; companies are your call (the industry is shown as a hint).
        4. Weight 0.00–1.00 is your judgment. "Use suggested weights" fills HOOT's generic default; change any you disagree with.
        5. Validate & add: rejected rows come back with a reason. Every save is backed up and logged.
